Method

  1. Collect posts and comments from app-store reviews, Reddit, the Google Photos Community, YouTube comments, and StackExchange.
  2. Clean and de-duplicate the raw items, then reconstruct individual posts/comments into full threads.
  3. Screen every thread with a cheap LLM pass: is this actually about finding or browsing old photos, as opposed to e.g. editing, sharing, or storage?
  4. Hand-code every flagged thread against a 32-code codebook — what was tried, what failed, how (or whether) it got resolved.
  5. Extract each coded thread into a structured episode with an exact evidence quote, then check a sample against a second, independent coder.
  6. Cluster episodes into problem areas and rank each one by frequency × severity × cleanliness.
